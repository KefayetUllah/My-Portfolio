How to Get 4x ROAS from Meta Ads

December 5, 2024 5 min read 3.1K views

After managing over $500K+ in Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad spend for e-commerce brands, I've developed a framework that consistently delivers 4x+ ROAS.

Here's the exact playbook I use — broken down into actionable steps you can implement today.

Step 1: Audience Strategy — Cast a Net, Then Narrow

Most brands make the mistake of targeting too narrowly from the start. Here's my approach:

  • Week 1-2: Broad targeting (interests-based) to gather data
  • Week 3-4: Lookalike audiences based on purchasers
  • Week 5+: Retargeting + dynamic creative optimization

💡 Pro Tip: Let Facebook's algorithm do the heavy lifting. Start broad, let it find your buyers, then scale what works.

Step 2: Creative Testing — 3-2-2 Method

I test 3 creative concepts, 2 headlines, and 2 primary texts. Here's the breakdown:

  • Concept A: Lifestyle / UGC style
  • Concept B: Product demonstration
  • Concept C: Customer testimonial / social proof

Step 3: Campaign Structure

Simple is better. Here's my go-to structure:

          🔹 CBO Campaign ($100/day)
              ├── Ad Set 1: Broad Interest (TOFU)
              ├── Ad Set 2: Lookalike 1% (MOFU)
              └── Ad Set 3: Retargeting (BOFU)

Step 4: Daily Optimization

Here's what I check every morning:

  • CTR: Below 1%? Kill the creative
  • CPC: Above $0.80? Adjust targeting
  • ROAS: Below 2x? Check funnel or creative fatigue
  • Frequency: Above 3? Refresh creative

Step 5: Scale Smart

When you find a winner, don't just double the budget overnight. Scale in 20% increments every 2-3 days. This prevents the "death spiral" where performance drops after a budget increase.

Final Thoughts

Consistent 4x ROAS isn't about luck — it's about testing, measuring, and optimizing. Follow this framework, and you'll be ahead of 90% of advertisers.
