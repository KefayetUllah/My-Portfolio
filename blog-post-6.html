Instagram Reels: The Secret to 10x Reach

November 15, 2024 3 min read 5.6K views

Instagram's algorithm has changed — and Reels are now the most powerful way to get discovered on the platform.

I've helped brands go from 500 views to 50,000+ views per Reel using these strategies. Here's exactly how to do it.

The Algorithm Shift

Instagram is now prioritizing:

  • Original content (reposts get less reach)
  • Watch time (longer views = more reach)
  • Engagement signals (likes, comments, shares, saves)
  • Audio usage (trending sounds get more push)

📱 Key Insight: Instagram wants to compete with TikTok. That means they're heavily favoring Reels in the algorithm. Don't sleep on this.

5 Reels Strategies That Work

1. Hook in the First 3 Seconds

You have 3 seconds to capture attention. Start with a visual hook, a question, or a bold statement. Text overlays are essential.

2. Use Trending Audio

Instagram pushes Reels that use trending sounds. Check the "Trending" tab in the Reels audio library and use songs with increasing popularity.

3. Add Captions + Text Overlays

70% of Reels are watched without sound. Add captions and on-screen text so people can follow along even on mute.

4. Keep It Under 30 Seconds

Shorter Reels get more complete views, which signals engagement to the algorithm. Aim for 15-25 seconds.

5. Post 3-5 Reels Per Week

Consistency matters. Post at least 3 Reels per week to stay visible in the algorithm.

Bonus: Repurpose to Multiply Your Reach

  • Share to Stories: Post your Reel to Stories with a "Share" sticker
  • Post to Feed + Reels: Check both boxes when posting
  • Repost to Other Platforms: Share to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, etc.

Final Thoughts

Reels aren't just a trend — they're the future of Instagram. Brands that master Reels will win the attention war. Start posting today.
